# Forty 2

Forty 2 is 42 Paris's summer campus, located on the Île d'Oléron (Charente-Maritime) at the Fort des Saumonards. Situated 100 meters from the beach, it offers bookable stays of 4 to 15 days from April to October.
Bookings are made at [book.forty2.fr](http://book.forty2.fr). For any questions, feel free to contact hello@forty2.fr

## 🏰 The Fort's history

---

Before becoming Forty2, it was the Fort des Saumonards. Construction began in 1810 under Napoleon's impulse, meant to strengthen French naval defenses alongside Fort Boyard. Completed in 1818, despite renovations it was quickly outpaced by military progress and was decommissioned in the early 20th century. Over the years it served as a prison, a squat, and even a holiday camp. Bought by 42 in 2018, the Fort des Saumonards is writing a new chapter of its history as Forty2.

## 😎  Your stay at Forty 2

---

**Stays at Forty 2 are offered over four days (a weekend), one week, or two weeks. Arrivals take place on Mondays and Thursdays. All stays include:**
- transport by bus or boat from La Rochelle's SNCF train station 🚌,
- all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) prepared by the Fort's chef 🥘,
- an individual bed in a dormitory 🛌,
- access to the cluster and to relaxation spaces (common room, reading corner…) 🖥️,
- the possibility of enjoying the activities available (beach volleyball, ping-pong, bikes…) 🚲
- and a great atmosphere!

## ⛵ Transport between La Rochelle and Forty2 

To get to Forty2, you have two options: coming by car 🚗 or taking the shuttle from La Rochelle 🛥️
By car, you'll be expected at the Fort between 4 p.m. and 6 p.m. The return trip takes place in the morning.
For the shuttle, 42 can organize your trip between La Rochelle and Forty 2 on the Île d'Oléron.
To take advantage of it, simply fill out the form sent with your booking confirmation, [available here](https://tally.so/r/kdWEGR).

# 🍳 Catering

---

Meals are varied, balanced, and adapted to allergies and dietary restrictions (halal, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free…). The chef prepares homemade dishes using local, seasonal produce. *Some of the vegetables even come from our own vegetable garden* 🥕
**Dining hall 🍽️**
The dining hall is freely accessible 24/7, giving you access to water fountains and coffee machines (cappuccino, café au lait, tea, espresso…). Depending on the weather (or your mood), you can eat inside or enjoy the sun by eating outside. Don't worry about sunburn, parasols are available for you to use.

The Fort has just over 200 beds spread across 7 single-gender dormitories of 30 people each. When booking, you can choose a top or bottom bunk.
Each bed is paired with two lockers that lock with a combination padlock: a large one, wardrobe-style, for clothes, and a small one near your bed for personal belongings. You'll also find a light (with different colors), a USB outlet, and ventilation there. Sheets are provided.

# 🟢 Accessibility 

---

At Forty2, everyone should be able to fully enjoy their stay. The venue was designed to be accessible to people with disabilities (PSH), in compliance with current standards. Several accommodations have been made to ensure comfort and independence for everyone:
- **Capsule-style accessible restrooms** (90 cm wide, 1.80 m long, with a front opening)
- **Adapted common areas**: accessible restrooms, step-free showers, grab bars for extra safety

*And of course, we remain available for any specific request or need.*
**Booking accessible restrooms:**
Specifically adapted cabins are available for people with disabilities. They are equipped to ensure maximum comfort and independence:
- A widened space to make moving around easier
- An entrance adapted for wheelchairs

**How do I book one?**
Simply send an email to **hello@forty2.fr**, specifying your situation and specific needs.
These cabins are primarily reserved for people with **medical proof** or **RQTH status** (recognized disabled worker status). Please let us know about your needs as soon as you first get in touch.

> ⚠️ Accessibility equipment is strictly reserved for those who need it. Any misuse may result in sanctions!

### Assistance
- If you need assistance when you arrive at FORTY2, no problem! A team member can help you get settled — just let us know in advance.
- **Guide dogs** and **assistance animals** are of course welcome at the Fort. Here too, a heads-up beforehand is always appreciated so we can welcome you in the best conditions.

#  ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

---

## **How do I register?**
  All 42 students can request to register via the form [available here](https://book.forty2.fr/login). You will get a response within 48 hours.
## **How are the dormitories organized?**
  The dormitories are organized with individual beds, each with two lockers for storing your belongings.
  Currently, Forty 2's dormitories are single-gender.
## **How do meals work?**
  Balanced meals are prepared every day by our chef. Breakfast is served from 8:30 to 10:00 a.m., lunch from 12:30 to 2:00 p.m., and dinner from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m.
  Before you come to the Fort, you will receive a questionnaire asking you to share any allergies or dietary requirements. We will do our best to accommodate them.

[More FAQ available here](https://ft42.notion.site/Forty-2-Q-A-a835909f162e49a7b35bc39982370f6f?source=copy_link).
## **What should I bring?**
  So you can travel light, we provide the sheets. However, remember to bring with you:
  - A shower towel
  - A beach towel & swimsuit
  - Sunscreen
  - A light sweater, as evenings can get cool
  - A pair of flip-flops to access the restrooms
  - A pair of not-too-dirty shoes / slippers to access the dormitories
  - Mosquito repellent in case you're sensitive to bites
  - Your best smile
## **What activities are on offer?**
  Besides the option of continuing your curriculum in the cluster, several activities are freely available: beach volleyball, ping-pong tables, foosball, Mölkky, pétanque…
  It's also possible to borrow bikes for the day to explore the Île d'Oléron.
## **How do I contact the Forty 2 team?**
  Got a question? Send us an email at hello@forty2.fr!
