# Inappropriate Behavior / Social Report

*I am a victim or witness of an assault, harassment, or any other inappropriate behavior: I immediately contact *[*social-report@42paris.fr*](mailto:social-report@42paris.fr)*.*

### ***What is the Social Report?***

---

*The Social Report is a dedicated email address that allows students, staff, or external contributors to report problematic situations within the campus.*
*This may include inappropriate behavior, conflicts between students, social difficulties, or any other situation that could affect the well-being or safety of the community.*
*This address serves as a confidential channel to alert the appropriate team so that suitable action can be taken.*

### ***Who receives messages sent to the Social Report?***

---

*Emails sent to this address are received by a limited number of staff members:*
- ***Sophie Viger**** – CEO of 42*
- ***Grégoire Martinez**** – Director of 42 Paris*
- ***Sanaé Blanc**** – Director of Communication / in charge of diversity*
- ***Floriane Raffaelli**** – Pedagogical Coordinator*

*These recipients are specifically designated to handle social, behavioral, or interpersonal matters.*
***Important:**** The list of people with access to the Social Report inbox is intentionally limited to ensure confidentiality and protect those reporting or involved.*

### ***How is a report handled?***

---
1. ***Email reception****
An authorized staff member reads the message and assesses the urgency or severity of the situation.*
1. ***Report analysis****
The Social Report team:*
  - *cross-checks information if necessary,*
  - *identifies the people involved,*
  - *assesses immediate risks,*
  - *determines whether other parties should be involved (security, legal advisor, etc.).*
1. ***Contact***
  *Depending on the situation, staff may:*
  - *contact the person who made the report (if contact details were provided),*
  - *interview the individuals involved,*
  - *collect additional testimonies.*
  *Testimonies may be ****anonymized**** when necessary.*
1. ***Implementation of actions***
  *Depending on the case, several types of actions may be taken:*
  - *mediation,*
  - *reminder of internal rules,*
  - *disciplinary action,*
  - *social, medical, or psychological support,*
  - *improvements to conditions or spaces,*
  - *implementation of preventive measures.*

> 
  *All information received via the Social Report is handled in a ****strictly confidential**** manner. Only authorized individuals have access to reports. Exchanges and case details are never disclosed beyond what is strictly necessary to resolve the situation.*
