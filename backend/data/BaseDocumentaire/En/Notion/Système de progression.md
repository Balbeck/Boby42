# Progression System

*Progression in the 42 cursus is based on defined rythme. You will find below all the details based on the platform you’re using.*

# *⏳ The YAMS System*
### *YAMS (42Next)*
  *The ****"YAMS" ****system is a pedagogical time-management tool for your training. It is designed to ensure your minimum engagement and the overall efficiency of your journey at 42.*
  > *⚠️ ****Warning:**** Failure to comply with the time constraints imposed by this system results in a ****"Drop Out"**** status, leading to permanent exclusion from 42 Paris.*
  ***The Objective:**** You must complete your ****Common Core**** within a specific timeframe determined by the YAMS system.*
  ### *How your progression works*
  ---
  *You progress by completing learning activities (individual projects, group projects, exams, etc.) organized into “modules.” Successfully completing these activities allows you to acquire skills, the ****“Skills.”***
  *The system also gives you visibility on the estimated time you will spend on each milestone (based on your progress so far) as well as a reference duration (calculated on the basis of 40 hours of work per week).*
  *There are then different levels:*
  - ***Outstanding Pace***
  - ***Great Pace***
  - ***Right On Target***
  - ***You’re Better Than That***
  - ***Significant Lag (or Grace Period):**** the final stage before dropout, see below*
  > 
    *The target is calculated based on 40 hours of work per week and completion of the Common Core in 15 months. If you plan to take more time, you will probably fall into the “You’re Better Than That” category, but that does not prevent you from completing the program!*
  ### *Deadline Mechanics*
  ---
  - ***Validation:**** Acquiring Skills allows you to validate progression steps called ****"Milestones"****.*
  - ***Extending the Deadline:**** Successfully completing a Milestone within the given timeframe allows you to push back your final deadline and avoid a Drop Out situation.*
  - ***Grace Period:**** In the event of a significant delay in completing a Milestone, you will be placed in a ****"Grace Period"**** lasting four weeks.*
  > 
    ***Warning:**** If you do not complete your current Milestone before this ****"Grace Period"**** expires, you will fall into ****Drop Out**** status.*
  *The YAMS system automatically determines your Milestone deadlines and your final Common Core completion date based on the pedagogical parameters defined by 42. Once you have validated all the modules that make up the Common Core, you will reach the rank of ****"Transcender"****.*

## *The Paced System (Intra V2/V3)*
  The Paced System is the progression system of Intra V2/V3. It gives you the opportunity to have multiple goals and a curriculum that adapts to your progress.
  ### *The Advantages*
  ---
  *This system provides:*
  - ***Good visibility on your progress:**** you can see when you’ll be working on each project, which can make it easier to find teammates for group projects.*
  - ***Strong consistency:**** it sets deadlines that are appropriate to the time spent on projects.*
  - ***A less stressful system:**** the countdown is much less harsh, and there’s greater flexibility regarding each person’s pace and situation.*
  ### *Milestones*
  ---
  - ***Available paces:**** several rhythms - called paces - are available: 8, 12, 15, 18, with 22 and 24 being the final ones.*
  - ***How it works:**** the system grants you additional days each time you validate a ****Milestone**** / a circle.*
  - ***Calculation:**** the 7 milestones of the core curriculum are calculated based on your program start date, your freezes, and the pace you are on.*
  ### *Key Indicators*
  ---
  - ***Common Core ETA:**** The target date set for you to finish the Common Core.*
  - ***Milestone Deadline:**** The target date set for the end of your current circle.*
  ### *System Dynamics*
  ---
  *If you are unable to validate your Milestone Deadline, you will change Pace and your Common Core ETA will be shifted.*
  - *If you reach the final Pace, a ****“Milestone Ultimate Deadline”**** will be displayed in ****red**** on your Intra.*
  - *On the other hand, if you complete your Milestone before your deadline, an automatic proposal will be made to switch to a faster Pace.*
  ### *Milestone Validations*
  ---
  *If you complete a Milestone according to the first objective you were given, you will earn ****“Bonus Days”****: these are free days that push back your deadline.*
  *You can change your pace if you don't validate the circle in time, or conversely, if you move faster (****speed up****). If you fail to reach your first objective, other deadlines will be proposed to you, until a Black Hole date appears in red. If this final deadline is not met, you will then be ****Black Hole****.*
  ### *Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)*
  ---
  **How does "Freeze" work with the Paced System?**
  *Your Pace (progression rhythm) is paused for the entire duration of your freeze. To understand the exact impact on your Milestones, please consult the **[Freeze Rules]**.*
  **Where can I see my current Pace?**
  *You can check your Pace and deadlines in real-time directly on **Intra V3**.*
  **Are the Freeze rules the same as Bonus Days?**
  *No. You can use your Freeze days whenever you want via the usual tool.*
