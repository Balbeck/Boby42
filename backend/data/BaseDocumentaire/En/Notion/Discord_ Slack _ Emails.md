# Discord, Slack & Emails

*We can’t say it enough… ****read your emails and Discord/Slack messages**** !*

## *Discord*

---

*Join the 42 Paris discord by logging in at *[discord.42paris.fr](http://discord.42paris.fr/) .
*The ****#staff**** channel allows you to communicate with the staff. Ask your questions there, but ****only after checking with others first****.*
*Want to report an issue on a workstation? Make sure to mention its ****full name****, such as f0r1s1 (cluster, row, seat). Without this information, there’s not much we can do… *

> 
  *All discussions on Discord must remain professional at all times. A code of conduct is included in the Internal Regulations.*

## *Slack*

---

*As a 42 Paris student, you can also join the 42 workspace: ****42beyondthecode****, which allows you to connect and interact with students from the entire network!*
*To do this, you need to create a Slack account using your student email address *`login@student.42.fr`* or *`fistname.lastname@learner.42.tech`* and your *`login`* as your full name.*
*To access the Slack workspace, follow this link: *[https://42beyondthecode.slack.com/](https://42beyondthecode.slack.com/)

## *Emails*

---

*For the most important communications, we also send emails to the address you provided when applying. *
*Finally, you will receive the Towel Times every Monday - the internal newsletter of 42 Paris -which contains all the important news, a list of upcoming events, and information about campus space availability for the next two weeks.*
