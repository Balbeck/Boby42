# The 42 Start-Up Club

*The ****42 Start-Up Club**** is the entrepreneurial community of 42 Paris, run by the school’s staff. It brings together students and alumni who are building, launching, or considering starting a startup.*
*The goal: create connections, share opportunities, and help projects grow through the strength of the 42 network.*
*🔢 ****Key figures:***
- ***525+ members**** in the community*
- *1 monthly newsletter with tips and resources*
- ***1 talk**** per month*
- *1 visit to STATION F per month*

*🚀 ****Who can join?***
*All 42 students or alumni who have a startup, a startup project, or simply want to explore the world of entrepreneurship!*
*✅ ****How to join the 42 Start-Up Club?***
*It’s simple - just fill out the form: *[*https://s.42paris.fr/42-startup-club*](https://s.42paris.fr/42-startup-club)* and you’re in 🤌*
