### **📌 Understanding the work-study program**
## **What is a work-study program (alternance)?**
  A **work-study program** is a training system based on alternating practical and theoretical phases.
  In practice, you are an **employee of a company** while continuing your training at 42.

  Your time is therefore split between:
  - periods in the company, with professional assignments
  - periods dedicated to your training and to validating your progress at 42
  The goal is to allow you to gain both:
  - real hands-on experience in a company
  - a State-recognized diploma or professional title, registered with the **RNCP**
  This program, as well as the associated projects on the intra, is specific to France. It is therefore only available on French campuses.

## **Why choose a work-study program?**
  - **You are employed and paid** during your training
  - Pay is generally **more advantageous than an internship**
  - You gain genuine professional experience over time
  - The pathway allows you to obtain a **State-recognized title**: RNCP level 6 or level 7 depending on the curriculum followed and subject to meeting the conditions for obtaining it.
  - With work-study status, you contribute to your retirement pension
## **Apprenticeship contract or professionalization contract?**
  These are the two main types of work-study contracts in France.
  Depending on your situation, one or the other may be offered — feel free to contact us to discuss your situation:
  - For the apprenticeship contract: You must be between 16 and 29 years old when the contract is signed, regardless of the contract's duration (no age limit for workers recognized as having disabled worker status, high-level athletes, or future business creators). If you are 30 or older, exceptions are possible under certain conditions. Contact us.
  - For the professionalization contract: No age limit, if you are 30 or older, provided you are a jobseeker at the time of the request.
### **✅ Eligibility conditions**
Access to a work-study program depends on **three elements**: your academic level, your administrative situation, and the requested contract format.

## 1. School eligibility conditions
  The work-study program is not accessible to all students.
It requires a sufficient level of progress, without having gone beyond the threshold past which it would no longer be necessary to obtain the diploma.
  You may not be eligible if:
  - you haven't yet reached the required prerequisites
  - or you are already too advanced in your curriculum
## 2. French legislation eligibility conditions
  You must be:
  - a national of an EEA country **or**
  - the holder of a residence permit authorizing this type of contract
  For non-EU students, a student visa will not be sufficient; you will need to go through a visa modification process for a work visa with the company that wishes to hire you on a work-study contract when the contract is set up.
## **3. Contract duration and required level**
  **There are two contract durations offered by the school:**
  ### 📅 2-year contract
  - **RNCP level 6 title**: starting from **level 9** with Common Core validated
  - **RNCP level 7 title**: starting from **level 14**, without Work Experience
  ### 📅 1-year contract
  - **RNCP level 6 title**: starting from **level 12**
  - **RNCP level 7 title**: starting from **level 16**
  For the 1-year contract: a Work Experience I project must have been validated, regardless of the title being prepared.
## **4. Does the apprenticeship have to be done with a single company?**
  The apprenticeship must be done with a single company. The goal is for the company to support you until you obtain your diploma. A contract termination is possible, as with any employment contract; our role is prevention and mediation to avoid this kind of situation.
  Please note:
  If a contract termination occurs, notify your Work-Study Manager as soon as possible, explaining the reasons for the termination.
  If you wish to continue with another work-study contract, you will need to find another host company; you will be supported by your Work-Study Manager for this.
## **5. If I am currently on a permanent contract (CDI), can I benefit from this type of contract?**
  Yes, a CDI can switch to an apprenticeship contract. The apprenticeship contract will put the CDI on hold, and the CDI will resume at the end of the apprenticeship contract.
## **6. Can I do a work-study program abroad?**
  No, this is not possible.
### **📅 School/company presence rhythm**
2 weeks in the company and 1 week of training at the school
Please note:
- One company day = **7 hours of work** (if you have a 35h/week contract)
- One school day = **7 hours of presence on campus**
- The school day calendar must be strictly followed; you must complete the number of hours planned in the calendar (no more, no less).
### 📚 Training time at school
The number of in-person training hours at school is 600h/year for RNCP Titles 39783 & 39774 awarded by 42 (i.e. 85 days of training at school at a rate of 7h/day + 5h on the last day).
### **📍 Presence and attendance during school days once the contract is signed**
You keep access to the 42 Paris campus under the same conditions as other students.
However, you are also bound by an **employment contract** with a company. You must therefore respect both your academic and professional obligations.
School days represent **7 hours of training per day between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.**
⚠️ Time outside this window is not counted.
A day of training at 42 is equivalent to a day of work.
Any absence, justified or not, must be reported to both the school and the company, with supporting documentation.
The school will inform the company in case of absence.
An unjustified absence may lead to a salary deduction or disciplinary measures according to the applicable rules.
All corrections, submissions, and project starts **related to the ongoing title** must be carried out **during the hours and days planned as part of the work-study program**.
Otherwise, these actions may be cancelled.

### **Can we do our 42 projects remotely?**
NO, 42 days must imperatively and entirely be done in person.
### 📝 Company evaluations
Your company will need to evaluate you at least 2 times over the course of the contract, or more depending on the duration of your work-study program.
It gives rise to a possible XP gain proportional to the time elapsed since the previous evaluation.
There is also a final evaluation, allowing you to complete your company XP associated with the apprenticeship project.
### 🎓 Educational objectives and follow-up
The educational requirements depend on the **RNCP title being prepared (see the RNCP Titles FAQ)**
By the end of the contract, all conditions required to obtain the targeted title must be validated.
Check-in points with the pedagogical team may be organized to review your progress and support you.
### **🏆 Diplomas prepared at 42 Paris and conditions for obtaining them**
## **What diploma will I get at the end of this contract?**
  A work-study contract must necessarily be linked to a **State-recognized RNCP title**.
  At 42 Paris, you can prepare one of the following two titles:
  - **RNCP Title 39783 — Computer Solutions Designer and Developer**
    - RNCP Level 6 Title, equivalent to a Bachelor's degree (accessible without a high school diploma)
    - 2 specializations to choose from:
      - Web & mobile development
      - Applied development

  - **RNCP Title 39774 — IT Architecture Expert**
    - RNCP Level 7 Title, equivalent to a Master's degree (accessible without a high school diploma)
    - 2 specializations to choose from:
      - Information systems and network architecture
      - Data architecture
    These 2 diplomas are State-recognized, accessible without any prior diploma requirement, and closely aligned with the skills acquired during training and sought by companies.

  ➡️ For more information, read:
  - Title 39783 FAQ [https://meta.intra.42.fr/articles/rncp-6-certificate](https://meta.intra.42.fr/articles/rncp-6-certificate)
  - Title 39774 FAQ [https://meta.intra.42.fr/articles/rncp-7-certificate](https://meta.intra.42.fr/articles/rncp-7-certificate)
## **What are the conditions for the diploma to be awarded?**
  To obtain your diploma, you must validate all the prerequisites of the **RNCP title being prepared** during your work-study program.

You can check the prerequisites on GCC.

### **📈 XP and company evaluations**
  The intra generally awards XP **twice a year**, provided the required evaluations are completed on time by your company mentor.
  ⚠️ Stay alert on this point: remember to follow up with your mentor if necessary and contact us quickly in case of a blocker.
  ---
### **📝 Diploma validation**
  Validation is done through **continuous assessment**: there is no final exam.
  You continue your curriculum normally, alongside your work-study program, with one simple goal: reach the prerequisites of the targeted title.
  At the end of the process, the staff will register you for the **certification jury** corresponding to your diploma.

  - your Apprenticeship project cannot be validated
  - an extension may be considered
  - or a return to the standard curriculum may be discussed with the agreement of the pedagogical team

### **💰 Pay and funding**
## **What will my pay be?**
  The minimum pay is set by law. There is no maximum. It's up to you to negotiate with the company. You can use tools such as the APEC salary simulator to better position yourself on the job market. [https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2918](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2918)
## **How is the training funded?**
  The cost of the training is fully covered by the host company's OPCO. This allows the company to fund your training without spending any money at all.
  Your training is funded, you earn a living at the company, and at the same time you can validate your diploma… Everyone wins!
  Please note:
  The OPCO checks that the number of training hours in the contract is respected — make sure to complete the school hours planned in the work-study calendar.

You need to send an email to the team in charge of work-study programs at alternance@42paris.fr and put them in touch with the company. Once this introduction is made, they will handle negotiating the funding of the training cost and the contractual part. As soon as everything is confirmed, the team will send you the information needed to obtain the contract.
### And how do I register for the project on the intranet?
It's very easy: you can't :)
The 4 projects on the intranet, leading to the 2 RNCP titles over 1 or 2 years respectively, are not open for registration.
It's only once your apprenticeship contract has been created and administratively validated that you will be registered by the campus staff on the corresponding project.

### **❌ What should I know in case of termination?**
In case of termination, your campus must allow you to continue your training for 6 months and help you find a new employer.
There are 4 possible termination situations:
- Contract termination during the trial period: Before 45 days
- Contract termination after the trial period
- Termination in case the apprentice is put in danger
- Termination due to non-compliance with the employment contract, under labor law.

### **📌 Rights and duties of the apprentice**
The apprentice's rights:
The apprentice benefits from the same rights as the company's employees, as well as legal provisions specific to their apprentice status:
- Benefit from employee status and a trial period of 45 days, consecutive or not, of presence in the company
- Benefit from social protection like all employees, including during time spent at school
- Accrue their training years as full years counting toward retirement
- Benefit from employer coverage of home/workplace public transport costs at 50%, under the same conditions as other employees
- Benefit from paid leave to be taken during periods in the company (same rights as the company's other employees, i.e. at least 25 working days for a full year)

The apprentice's duties:
- Register at the university or school providing the training
- Get involved in the training as well as in the company
- Comply with the internal rules of the company and the training establishment
- Keep the follow-up logbook up to date (exchanges as part of the apprenticeship)
- Submit, where applicable, absence justifications as defined by labor law. Other absences are considered unjustified and may result in sanctions, primarily a salary deduction.
