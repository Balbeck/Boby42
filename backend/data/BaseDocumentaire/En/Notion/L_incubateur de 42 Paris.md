# The 42 Paris Incubator

*Located at STATION F, the 42 Paris Incubator supports entrepreneurs from the entire 42 network in launching and scaling their projects 🚀*

### ***Program & Support***

---

***What does the program include?***
- *3 to 4 monthly workshops led by experts (sales, product, go-to-market, fundraising, etc.)*
- *Group coaching*
- *1 personalized coaching session per month*
- *Access to the STATION F ecosystem*
- *Networking opportunities (experts, investors, partners)*
- *Cohort life with other startups*

***What is the duration of the program?
****9 months, with the possibility of renewal.*
***Is remote participation possible?
******No.**** The 42 incubator and STATION F are primarily physical spaces and communities. Founders must be based in Paris to be physically present at STATION F. It is therefore not relevant to apply if co-founders cannot make the incubator their main workplace.*
***Can founders have side activities alongside their startup?
******Strongly discouraged.**** We prioritize founders who are fully committed to their project. Building a startup requires speed, availability, and continuous iteration, which is difficult to combine with another activity. Partial involvement is therefore seen as a weak signal during application review.*
***Is there a Demo Day?
****Yes, at the end of the program, in front of investors.*

### ***Eligibility & Criteria***

---

***Who can apply?
****Any team building a startup with at least one co-founder from a 42 campus who has completed the Common Core.*
***Do all co-founders need to be from 42?
******No.**** Only ****one co-founder must be from 42**** (any campus) and have completed the Common Core. Other team members can come from different backgrounds.*
***Is my startup eligible if I only have an intern/employee from 42?
******No.**** We only consider applications from startups with a co-founder from 42, whether a student or an alumnus.*
***Can I apply before finishing the Common Core?
****Yes, you can apply right now, but admission will only be possible after completing your Common Core. Applications are open continuously, but entry is conditional on completing the Common Core, with no exceptions. We may also re-review your application if the project has evolved significantly between your first application and the end of the Common Core.*
***Can I apply solo?
******Yes.**** Solo founders are welcome.*
***At what stage should the project be?
****Ideally: MVP or early traction. Projects that are too early (just an idea without execution or user testing) are less likely to be selected.*

### ***Application Process***

---

***Are there specific intake dates?
******Yes.**** A main intake happens once a year (10–15 startups). The last intake was April 9, 2026. Rolling applications are also accepted depending on availability.*
***How to apply?
****Through the online application form*[* on the incubator page.*](/557b73716b6843acb8c8519699995d2c?pvs=25)
***Selection steps
****Application review → potential interviews → decision.*
***Evaluation criteria***
*The standard incubator selection criteria will be assessed:*
- *Team*
- *Execution ability*
- *Problem understanding*
- *Product quality*
- *Market potential*

***How long does it take?
****A few weeks on average.*
***Can I apply multiple times?
****Yes. Rejection is not final if the project evolves.*

### ***Business Model***

---

***Does the incubator invest or take equity?
******No.**** It is not a fund. You keep 100% ownership of your startup.*
***Are there fees?
******Yes, but reduced.***
- *First 9 months: ****€100 excl. tax/person/month***
- *Renewal: ****€250 excl. tax/person/month***

***Do you help with fundraising?
******We support and connect you—but execution is up to you.*** *Fundraising is not a prerequisite for joining the incubator. However, if it is a goal for you, we help you prepare for it in the best possible conditions. Concretely:*
- *Access to investor network (including regular visibility via newsletters)*
- *Access to STATION F ecosystem (+300 investors)*
- *Exposure to other fundraising startups*
- *Expert sessions (dilutive and non-dilutive funding)*
- *Final Demo Day*

*Goal: make you credible and ready when you decide to raise funds.*
***Can we share desks?******
No.**** The 42 incubator allocates workstations to members holding a badge/access granted by STATION F; these badges/access passes are personal and cannot be shared or lent.*

### ***Ecosystem & Opportunities***

---

***Can we join multiple STATION F programs simultaneously?
******No.**** Only one program at a time per startup and founding team.*
***Can we join after another STATION F program?
******Yes.**** A startup can also be transferred from one program to another, subject to the agreement of both programs and validation by the STATION F team.*
***Advantages of STATION F
****The largest startup campus in the world, with investors, startups, corporates, and events.*
***Access to investors?
****Yes, via both networks.*
***Networking opportunities?
****Yes (investors, partners, experts).*
***Recruit 42 students?
****Yes.*
***Perks?
****Yes (tools, credits, partners).*

### ***After the Program***

---

***What happens after 9 months?
****Structured support ends, but renewal for another 9 months is possible depending on progress.*
***Can we stay at STATION F?
****Yes:*
- *Renew incubation*
- *Join another program*
- *Rent offices independently*

***Alumni follow-up?
****Yes, via a Slack community.*

### ***Special Cases***

---

***Currently in apprenticeship (alternance)?
****Unfortunately, you’re not eligible.*
***SFP status?
****Please declare your status during your application to review conditions.*
***Currently employed?******
****You can apply, but must commit full-time if accepted.*
***Already raised funds?******
****Yes, this remains compatible. The program is suited to both early-stage startups and already-funded projects; we will take time to talk it through and make sure the incubator matches your startup's stage of progress.*
***Already in another incubator?***
- *Not possible within STATION F simultaneously*
- *Possible to join after your current program*
- *Possible if incubated outside STATION F, but better to wait until the end*
