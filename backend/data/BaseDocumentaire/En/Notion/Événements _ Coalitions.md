# Events & Coalitions

## *Events at 42 Paris 🎉*

---

*At 42 Paris, campus life is driven by ****more than 500 events per year****. They help you develop your skills, expand your network, and get involved in school life.*
*Everyone is encouraged to participate and contribute, at their own level, to keeping this collective dynamic alive.*

### Events types

---

*Events organized on campus fall into three main categories:*
- ***Student events**** organized by [student associations and clubs] of 42 Paris.*
- ***Events organized by 42 Paris****, either by the staff or in collaboration with partners: conferences, workshops, hackathons, AMAs, Transcendence meetups, social events, etc.*
- ***External events**** that take place off-campus and are shared by 42 Paris*

### *How to participate?*

---

*To participate in an event, registration is done via ****the intra**** or the ****LMS****.*

> 
  *Signing up for an event means committing to attend. Organizers (often students, associations, or external speakers) rely on your presence to ensure the event runs smoothly. Only sign up if you are available, and make sure to unregister in advance if something comes up. In case of unexcused absence, you may receive ****TIG**** (community service tasks).*

## *Coalitions*

---

*A coalition? 🧐 It’s a group you are assigned to throughout your entire time at 42.*
*Your goal: bring your coalition to life, participate in its events, and most importantly, earn as many points as possible to make it shine on campus.*

### *The Fédération 🟦, The Assembly 🟪, The Alliance 🟩, and The Order 🟧*

---

*At 42 Paris, there are four student coalitions:*
- *The Fédération 🟦*
- *The Assembly 🟪*
- *The Alliance 🟩*
- *The Order 🟧*

*You will be assigned to a coalition after your admission. This assignment is automatic and stays with you throughout your time at 42.*
*The goal remains the same: help your coalition win by accumulating as many points as possible. These points can be earned in different ways:*
- *by participating in events organized on campus*
- *by winning challenges or inter-coalition activities*
- *by getting involved in student life (projects, initiatives, activities)*
- *during certain special events where points are at stake*
